# AI & Data Skills Demand in the Maharashtra Job Market

## Project Overview

A data-driven analysis of **6,076 Maharashtra job postings** to identify demand for AI and data skills and emerging career requirements.

## Objectives

* Identify the most demanded AI and data skills.
* Analyze demand for Python, SQL, and Power BI.
* Examine AI-skill requirements in non-AI roles.
* Analyze job postings by location, experience, and work mode.
* Explore salary patterns across job postings.

## Tools & Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Statistical Analysis
* Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Performed

* Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
* Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
* Skill Frequency Analysis
* Statistical Data Analysis
* Job Market Trend Analysis
* Data Visualization

## Key Findings

* Analyzed **6,076 job postings** from the Maharashtra job market.
* **SQL and Python** were among the most frequently demanded skills.
* AI and data skills were also present in selected non-AI roles.
* Job requirements varied by **location, experience level, and work mode**.
